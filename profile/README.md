**Wayli is an audio walking-tour app.** You tell it what you are curious about and how long you
have; it puts together a walking route and tells each place's story as you arrive there. No group
to keep up with, no screen to read while you walk.

## How it works

  <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wayli-ai/.github/main/profile/assets/step-1-plan-dark.svg"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wayli-ai/.github/main/profile/assets/step-1-plan-light.svg" width="100%" alt="Step 1 of 3, Plan: tell it your time and what you like. **1. Plan.** Choose a place, what interests you and the time you have. You can preview the
chapters and the route before you leave.

<p align="center">
  <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wayli-ai/.github/main/profile/assets/step-2-walk-dark.svg"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wayli-ai/.github/main/profile/assets/step-2-walk-light.svg" width="100%" alt="Step 2 of 3, Walk: follow the line, look at the city. **2. Walk.** Go at your own pace. Pause, linger, skip a stop: the estimated times help you
choose, but the walk is yours.

<p align="center">
  <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wayli-ai/.github/main/profile/assets/step-3-listen-dark.svg"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wayli-ai/.github/main/profile/assets/step-3-listen-light.svg" width="100%" alt="Step 3 of 3, Listen: each stop is a chapter. **3. Listen.** Each stop is a narrated chapter that starts as you arrive. Audio keeps playing
with the screen off, and a walk can be downloaded for offline listening.

## Why we built it, and what we care about

A city is full of stories that are easy to walk straight past. A guidebook asks you to stop and
read; a group tour sets the pace and the route for you. We wanted a third way: the story arrives
when you do, in your ear, and the walk fits the hour you actually have.

<p align="center">
  <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wayli-ai/.github/main/profile/assets/values-dark.svg"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wayli-ai/.github/main/profile/assets/values-light.svg" width="100%" alt="What we care about: stories told where you stand; eyes up, phone in your pocket; built around your time; honest about how stories are made; respect for the place and its sources; your walk stays yours."></picture>
</p>

- **Stories told where you stand.** A chapter is about the place in front of you, and it starts when you reach it.
- **Eyes up.** Wayli is made to be heard, not watched. Follow the line at a glance and keep the phone in your pocket.
- **Built around your time.** A walk is assembled around your interests, your pace, the time you have and where you start.
- **Honest about how stories are made.** Stories are not generated on the spot. Chapters are written and checked by people, then narrated, ahead of time; what Wayli assembles for you is the route. Where a city has no stories yet, the site says so.
- **Respect for the place and its sources.** Stories are about real places. On our site each photo carries its author and licence, and the maps are OpenStreetMap.
- **Your walk stays yours.** Your position is used on your device, after you allow it, and no history of where you have been is kept. No ad network shows ads on Wayli, personal data is not sold, and it is not used to train AI models. The details are in the [privacy policy](https://wayli.ai/privacy/).
